What's New

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed a crash that could happen when you closed a popped-out pane's window, such as a browser, to dock it back in. The workspace no longer collapses to the docked pane's width and pulls the main window below its minimum size, which used to start a resize loop that crashed the app.
  • The main window now stays at or above its minimum size even when its contents try to make it narrower or shorter.
