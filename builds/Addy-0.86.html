What's New

New Features

  • Choose your addy.land username during onboarding. A new "Choose a Username" step comes right after sign-in, prefilled with the handle your account got automatically. Pressing Next saves any change.
  • A new Settings → Account page shows who is signed in and lets you change your addy.land username. It also has a "Manage Account on addy.land" button and a Sign Out button that asks for confirmation first.
  • The username field gives live feedback as you type. It lowercases your entry, strips a leading "@", and flags invalid characters or lengths right away (1–24 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, no leading or trailing hyphen). After a short pause it checks with addy.land whether the name is available. If addy.land refuses a save, you see its reason.

Improvements

  • Closing the setup window no longer quits Addy just because you haven't finished the username step. Only signing in is required.
