What's New

New Features

  • Addy Mobile now supports a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It shows up to three of your Mac's chat agents that are working, waiting on you, or just finished, with the most urgent first.
  • Permission prompts and questions from an agent appear in the Live Activity with up to two answer buttons, so you can respond without opening the app. Free-text questions still need the app.
  • The Live Activity keeps updating when your phone isn't connected to the Mac, using push updates through the relay. It starts when a burst of work begins and ends once every agent has been idle for a short while.
  • You can send photos and files from the phone along with a message, up to 5 MB in total. Images are attached as images and everything else as a file.
  • Images an agent shows, such as screenshots or pictures it links from disk, now appear inline in the phone's transcript. Only the newest images are sent in full. Older ones in a long transcript fall back to their alt text.
  • You can close a pane on your Mac from the phone, which ends an agent's session as the close button does.

Improvements

  • The pairing approval panel now uses the same animated splash background as the phone (yellow, red and blue bands under film grain), so you can compare both screens while pairing. Dragging on the background pushes the bands like wet paint, and the background stays still if Reduce Motion is on. The panel's text was switched to white to stay readable on it.
  • Chat agents that haven't been named yet now show as "New Chat" on the phone. New panes and mode changes (chat agent versus terminal) show up on the phone right away, without waiting for the saved layout to catch up.

Internal Changes

  • The remote protocol was updated to version 10 to support attachments, transcript images, closing panes and Live Activity registration. Update the Mac app and Addy Mobile together.
